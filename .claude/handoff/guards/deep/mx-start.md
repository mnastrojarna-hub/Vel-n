Every cell names what the ORIGINAL look does and the guard record id(s) in `inv_mx-start.json`. The v2 verdict is in the last column of the record list below.

## The one finding that is not HOLDS

**mx-start-35 (WEAKER)** — Terms (VOP) and personal-data consent are not ticked (plus the guardian consent for a children's bike); the CTA is disabled with a hint. v2 evidence: lib/features/booking/v2/booking_form_view_v2.dart:246-257 (BookingConsentsV2), :173 (blocker v2BkBlockConsent); BUT lib/features/booking/booking_entry.dart:71-90 + lib/features/booking/v2/booking_carry_v2.dart:35-44 keep the whole previous draft, including ticked consents, when the customer comes back to the SAME bike before paying; lib/core/shell_tab_action.dart:51-52 (v2 Home tab does not clear the draft). Owner decision 2026-10-22 ("nic nevyplňovat dvakrát"). The check itself still runs, but the default differs: in v2 the boxes can already be ticked on a second visit to the same bike, so the customer may proceed without seeing them again. Another bike, another account or a paid booking resets them (booking_carry_v2.dart:20-25, 34).
